## 1. Introduction

LIFE OFFICE (the "App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles.

## 2. Information We Collect

The App **does not require account registration**. The following data is stored **on your device only** and is not sent to our servers (except for in-app purchases).

| Data | Purpose | Storage |
|------|---------|---------|
| Punch records (date/time, pre-clock-in notes, clock-out comments) | App functionality | On-device SQLite |
| Profile name | Home screen display and receipt printing | On-device storage |
| Settings (target time, work days, reminders, shake-to-punch, theme, barcode content) | App functionality | On-device storage |
| Receipt image saves | Saved to your photo library when you choose | Device Photos app |

Punch receipt barcodes are **generated on your device** from your profile name and punch date/time. They are not sent externally for generation or display.

## 3. Third-Party Services

| Service | Purpose | Provider |
|---------|---------|----------|
| RevenueCat / Apple App Store | In-app purchase processing and purchase status verification | RevenueCat, Inc. / Apple Inc. |

Information required for purchases is handled according to Apple and RevenueCat privacy policies:

- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## 4. Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

- **Notifications**: Punch reminders at scheduled times
- **Motion**: Shake-to-punch detection
- **Photo Library**: Saving punch receipts

## 5. Tracking

The App does not perform advertising tracking. App Tracking Transparency (ATT) is not used.

## 6. Data Deletion

Deleting the App from your device removes all data stored on the device.
